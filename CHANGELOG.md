# Changelog

## v0.9.0 - 2026-11-19

The Mac app, notarized, with a 30-day trial. Requires macOS 26 or later.

### Added
- **Capacity outlook.** Local trend and anomaly detection with a time-to-limit estimate for CPU, memory and disk.
- **Process inspector.** Open a process from an application to see its details.
- **CSV export of Applications.**
- **Menu bar disk and battery rows.**
